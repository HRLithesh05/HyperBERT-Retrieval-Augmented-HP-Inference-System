2.2 Approach2: Instruction-TuningforClassification
Our second approach frames the classification problem as a form of natural language instruction following recent
research[Wangetal.,2024,Fatemietal.,2024,Yinetal.,2024]. Insteadofextractingembeddings,weconverteach

onmanysuchprompt→answer pairssothatitlearnstoproducetheappropriatelabelwhengivenaqueryprompt.
Thisinstruction-tuningapproachfollowstheparadigmofrecentmulti-taskfine-tuningefforts(e.g. FLAN,T0)where

etal.,2021,Sanhetal.,2021]. Inourcase,the“instructions”aresimplyrequeststoclassifythegiventext,effectively
requiringthemodeltogeneratethecorrectlabeltoken,whichforalimitedlabelsetmightbeamoreindirectwayto
performclassification.
Wefine-tunetheLLMontheseprompt→answerpairsusingsupervisedlearning(standardnext-tokenpredictionlosson
thelabeltokens). SimilartoApproach1,weapply4-bitquantizationandLoRAadapters(r=64)toreducememory
