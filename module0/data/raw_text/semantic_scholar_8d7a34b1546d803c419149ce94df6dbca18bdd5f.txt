1 Introduction
Definitionsarecentraltothewayinwhichhumansconveyknowledgeaboutthemeaningofconcepts.
Accordingly, a large number of general and domain-specific dictionaries have been created. As new

Earlyattemptstosolvethistaskreliedonrule-basedmethods(KlavansandMuresan,2001;Cuietal.,

Reiplingeretal.,2012;Jinetal.,2013). Whilebeingabletoidentifyawiderrangeofdefinitions,these

hasshiftedtoneuralnetworkbasedmodels(Espinosa-AnkeandSchockaert,2018;Veysehetal.,2019).
The method we analyze in this paper is based on fine-tuning a pre-trained BERT language model
(Devlin et al., 2018). This strategy has recently proven successful across a wide range of Natural
