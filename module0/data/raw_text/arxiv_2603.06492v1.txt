WeintroducedNOBLE(NonlinearlOw-rankBranchforLinearEnhancement),afamilyofnonlinear
low-rankbranchesfortransformerlinearlayers. Amongseveralactivationfunctions,wefindCosNet,

Our experiments demonstrate that NOBLE benefits a broad range of tasks: LLM autoregressive
modeling,BERTmaskedlanguagemodeling,autoregressiveimagetokenmodeling,andViTimage

from NOBLE, suggesting the conflict is with the augmentation strategy rather than the task or
modality.
