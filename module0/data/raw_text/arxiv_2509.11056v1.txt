proposed two models, i.e., BERT and UBERT, to handle the
tokensequencetasks.Afterpre-training,theBERTmodelcan
be directly applied to systems with dynamic user counts and
canbeefficientlyfine-tunedtonewtasks.TheUBERTmodel,
empowered by element-wise tokenization, antenna encoding,
taskembedding,andpre-trainingwithmulti-tasklossfunction,
