8 BroaderImpactandEthics
Automated fact-checking systems can provide substantial public benefit when they are used as assistive tools for
journalists,moderators,researchers,orendusers. Asystemthatcanretrieveevidencequickly,summarizerelevant
passages,andexposereasoningstructurecanreducetheburdenonhumanfact-checkersandhelpprioritizeclaimsfor

Forthisreason,weemphasizeevidencegrounding,explanationtransparency,anduncertaintyawarenessthroughout

This paper presented TRUST Agents, a collaborative multi-agent framework for fake news detection, evidence-
